The Problem

In emergencies, 70% of medical errors are caused by lack of communication. If you're unconscious or in shock, how will doctors know your allergies, blood type, or medications?

The Solution

PulseID provides a secure, instant link to your medical profile via a QR code or ID number. First responders get the data they need in seconds, not minutes.

Simplicity saves lives

How it Works

1. Store Your Data

Create your secure medical profile including allergies, medications, and emergency contacts.

2. Link to PulseID

Receive your unique PID and QR code that can be placed on your phone, wallet, or medical jewelry.

3. Scan in Emergency

First responders scan your code to instantly view critical info. The QR code links directly to your profile via a unique URL.
